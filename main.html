Death Note Summary

Death Note is a story about Light Yagami, a brilliant student who discovers a mysterious notebook that can kill anyone whose name is written in it. He decides to use its power to eliminate criminals and create a new world, but a brilliant detective named L begins to investigate him.

Actors

Light Yagami, L, Misa Amane, Ryuk and Near are important characters in Death Note.
